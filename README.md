Entry for the [Deadpan Forecasts for Cities That Do Not Exist](https://make.fun/bounty/u-coral-ibis-18-deadpan-forecasts-for-cities-that-do-151d4d8c) bounty on make.fun.

The entry is a single deadpan weather forecast, delivered in the flat tone of a TV weather presenter, for a city that does not exist: in Lower Muckleberry, the fog clears by noon, then returns for its afternoon shift.

`main.ts` writes the entry line to `/output/entry.txt`.
